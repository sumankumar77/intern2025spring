ARE YOU INTERESTED IN LEARNING ABOUT WAYS TO HELP REDUCE YOUR DIABETES WASTE?
The Florida State University College of Nursing and the Diabetes Technology Society are conducting a study to assess the medical waste produced during diabetes management. We are actively seeking participation from adults who use insulin and a continuous glucose monitor (CGM).

What would you do if choosing to participate in this study?

  • Reporting Medical Waste: You will be asked to report diabetes-related medical waste and quantity via a convenient online daily checklist.
  • Convenient Participation: All study activities can be carried out from home.
  • Study Duration: 30 days.
  • Compensation: $25 gift card after completing the study.

There is no cost for taking part in this study.
